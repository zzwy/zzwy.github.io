About

I am a Master's student in the School of Computer Science at Northwestern Polytechnical University. Before that, I received my undergraduate training from the School of Artificial Intelligence at Anhui University.

My current work focuses on computational imaging and low-level vision, especially HDR reconstruction/restoration, robust image restoration under complex degradations, multimodal image fusion, and generative priors for visual enhancement.

Research Interests

HDR Imaging

Multi-exposure HDR reconstruction, deghosting, RAW burst fusion, and perceptual HDR representation.

Image Restoration

Restoration under noise, blur, saturation, misalignment, low-light conditions, and mixed degradations.

Multimodal Fusion

Robust visual fusion using complementary modalities, multi-frame inputs, and semantic priors.

Generative AI

Diffusion and generative priors for low-level vision, enhancement, and high-fidelity reconstruction.
