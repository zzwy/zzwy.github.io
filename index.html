👋 About Me

Hi! I am Weiyu Zhou (周玮煜 in Chinese), a Master's student in the School of Computer Science at Northwestern Polytechnical University, advised by Prof. Qingsen Yan. I joined the Master's program in 2025 and expect to continue toward a Ph.D. through the direct M.S.-to-Ph.D. track in Fall 2026.

Before that, I studied Artificial Intelligence at Anhui University. My research interests include HDR imaging, image restoration, multimodal fusion, and generative artificial intelligence.
